How the model works

{% if model_kind == "hybrid" %}

The diagnosis comes from a hybrid CNN-Transformer (CAFormer-S18). Its first layers are convolutions, which are good at local texture such as spots, rings and discoloured veins; its later layers use self-attention, which relates distant parts of the leaf to each other.

  • It started from weights pre-trained on ImageNet-22k (14 million everyday photos), then was fine-tuned on PlantVillage lab photos plus PlantDoc field photos taken in real gardens and farms, with heavy augmentation (cropping, lighting, blur, occlusion) so it copes better outdoors.
  • It looks at your photo and its mirror image and averages the two answers.

    The diagnosis comes from a convolutional neural network (CNN): a type of model that learns visual patterns, such as spots, rings and discoloured veins, directly from example photos.

    • It was trained on the public PlantVillage dataset: tens of thousands of photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds.
      • {% endif %}
    • It chooses between {{ stats.classes }} classes: {{ stats.diseases }} diseases, {{ stats.conditions - stats.diseases }} "healthy" classes, and one "no leaf in the photo" class.
    • Your photo is resized and centre-cropped to 224 × 224 pixels, exactly as in training, then the network gives a probability for every class. The highest is the diagnosis.
    • If the top probability is below {{ "%.0f" | format(threshold * 100) }}%, we tell you the model isn't sure.
    • It runs on an ordinary server CPU in well under a second. Photos are processed in memory and never stored.

    Limitations

    • It can only recognise the conditions listed above. A disease it has never seen will be reported as the closest one it knows.
    • Most training photos were taken in a lab. Cluttered field photos, several leaves, or poor light still lower accuracy, so check the confidence and retake if unsure.
    • It looks at one leaf only. It can't see roots, stems, fruit, soil or weather, which an expert would consider.
    • Product suggestions come from the original project's data and haven't been independently checked.
